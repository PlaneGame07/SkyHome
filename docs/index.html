Legal & attributions

SkyHome is strictly for recreational use and situational awareness. It cannot be used as a primary navigation or tactical flight tool. Nothing in this app is checked against official aviation or weather sources and nothing here should be relied on for a real decision.

Map tiles: © OpenStreetMap contributors.
Weather: data by Open‑Meteo.com.
Aircraft photos: © Planespotters.net contributors, and Airport‑Data.com.
Aircraft type photos: Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons contributors.
Flight and route data: adsb.lol, adsbdb.com and the US National Weather Service AviationWeather.gov.

This app makes outbound requests directly to the services above from this device. Your approximate coordinates are sent to them so they can return local weather, nearby aircraft and map tiles. No separate server collects or stores this.
